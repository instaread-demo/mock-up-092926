Popular Voter Guide “Value Your Vote” Released

Monday, 28 September 2026, 3:53 pm
Press Release: Family First New Zealand

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 1:39
Instaread

Family First NZ has just released Value Your Vote 2026 for the upcoming General Election.

This is the seventh election for which we have provided this very popular, family-focused voting resource.

“Over the past two decades, there have been a number of law changes voted on by our politicians which have specifically impacted the welfare and wellbeing of New Zealand. Marriage and the role of parents have been increasingly devalued. Who we are as male and female has been confused and distorted. The respect for life has never been more at risk, and our children are being exposed to radical gender, sexuality, and critical theory ideology,” says Bob McCoskrie, CEO and founder of Family First.

The 24-page glossy pamphlet (and the online version on our website ValueYourVote.nz) allows families to see how each current MP has voted on important social issues that have come before Parliament.

There's also additional questions that we have put to the leaders of the major political parties.

Where do the party leaders stand on the issues of abortion, biological truth, legalising drugs, gender ideology, euthanasia, marriage, puberty blockers, critical race theory, radical sexuality and gender education in schools, the ‘conversion therapy’ law, social media and pornography, hate speech laws, and more?

We’ve also included an explainer on the voting system and what is known as the “wasted vote”, and commentary from CEO Bob McCoskrie and former National MP Simon O’Connor.

“Voting according to our values is the greatest freedom and privilege we have. But we need to know what the parties and candidates value before we vote for them.”
