Highland Council receives planning application for new gym and pilates studio in offices close to Inverness Harbour

By Philip Murray
- philip.murray@hnmedia.co.uk
Published: 14:11, 28 September 2026

A new gym could be coming to the Inverness Harbour area after plans were lodged with Highland Council.

Applicant Fased Gym Ltd is seeking permission to change the use of offices in two units at the In:Business Centre in Longman Drive.

If approved the change of use would enable gym facilities to operate from the site.

Drawings lodged with the application reveal that the gym would operate over two floors of 24 Longman Drive.

Several internal partition walls separating offices within the units would be removed to create two open-plan gym areas on the ground and first floors. A pilates studio is also planned for part of the ground floor.
